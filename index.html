Why "Four Bytes"?

பெயருக்குப் பின்னால்

One byte is small. On its own it barely holds a letter. But stack four together and you get thirty-two bits — enough to hold a whole number, a whole idea, a whole system.

That's the channel in one line: a small team, playing loud, building something that adds up. Every upload is one more byte in the stream — Tamil commentary, real reactions, no filters.

Whether it's a battle royale clutch or a three-hour story deep-dive, the goal stays the same: make gaming sound as good in Tamil as it feels to play.
